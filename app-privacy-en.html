Privacy and data storage

iPhone app (test version) · Updated November 9, 2026.

Routine Note is an app for recording the structure of your routine and reviewing your practice. There are no accounts. Your records, videos and audio stay on this iPhone and are not sent to any server.

What stays on this device

Routines, practice records, sequences, audio information, video/audio/recording files and settings are stored on this iPhone. They are never uploaded. They are lost if you delete the app or erase its data, so export important records with Settings → Full backup and keep the file somewhere else.

What is sent (feedback only)

Only when you send feedback, the type, your message, an optional name, the app version and device information are sent to Supabase, together with the time the server receives it. Practice records, videos and audio are never attached automatically. If sending fails, the message is handed to your mail app and you complete it there.

Camera, microphone and files

The app asks for camera or microphone access only when you record. Recorded video and audio are stored on this iPhone. Files are imported or exported only when you choose to. If you export to iCloud or another service, that service’s terms also apply. If other people appear in a video, get their consent (or a parent’s consent for minors).

Deleting data

Data on this device is removed with Settings → Reset or by deleting the app. Backups you exported remain where you saved them. To delete feedback you sent, contact us at the address below.

External services and retention

Supabase is used only to store feedback. The app contains no advertising or analytics. If you open an external web page, that page’s policy applies. Feedback is kept as long as it is needed to improve the app. Connection records such as IP addresses may remain in the service provider’s operational logs for a period of time. Processing by service providers may take place outside Japan.

Who it is for and changes

The current test version is for people aged 18 or over, or with a parent’s consent. If features or data handling change, this page will be updated.
